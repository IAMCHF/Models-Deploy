Apple Unveils Next-Generation M5 Chip with Breakthrough Neural Engine

At its annual Worldwide Developers Conference, Apple today announced the M5 system-on-chip, representing a significant leap in silicon performance and energy efficiency. Built on a second-generation 2-nanometer process, the M5 packs 40 billion transistors and features a redesigned 16-core Neural Engine capable of 38 TOPS of AI inference performance. The chip integrates a 10-core CPU and a 16-core GPU, delivering up to 50 percent faster processing compared to its predecessor while consuming 30 percent less power. Apple executives highlighted the M5's role in enabling on-device generative AI, allowing features like real-time image generation and advanced language processing to run entirely locally without cloud dependency. The new chip will debut in the redesigned MacBook Pro lineup this fall, with iPad Pro models following in early 2026. Industry analysts note that the M5 positions Apple competitively against Qualcomm's Snapdragon X Elite and AMD's Ryzen AI 300 series in the emerging AI PC market.
